## Highlights

The first release of bloodborne-linux: Bloodborne (CUSA03173, patch 1.09) running natively on Linux x86-64. The game's own code runs on your CPU; the PS4 system and GPU are translated. You need your own dump of the game; this package contains no game files.

**What works**

- The game from the title through character creation and the intro to the clinic, saving and continuing, and Central Yharnam, fights included.
- 30 FPS as on the PS4, and 60 or 120 FPS at normal game speed (120 where the GPU keeps up). The few differences from 30 FPS that remain are listed in `docs/design/frame-rate.md`.
- Rendering resolutions up to 3840x2160, FSR, anisotropic filtering, switches for motion blur and chromatic aberration, a field of view, and 21:9 and 32:9 with the HUD at 16:9. Every option defaults to the game's own look.
- Loading times like the PS4 emulator shadPS4, which this project builds on.
- A launcher that finds your dump, checks it and keeps settings, saves, caches and logs in the folder next to it.

**Getting started**

Unpack `Bloodborne-Linux-v0.1.0-x86_64.tar.gz` into a folder you can write to and start `bb-launcher`. Put your dump in `Bloodborne-Linux/game/` or point the launcher at it. Check the download with `sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`.

Needs glibc 2.31 or newer (Ubuntu 20.04, Debian 11, SteamOS 3 and later), a Vulkan 1.3 driver, and fontconfig for the launcher.

**Known problems**

- A playthrough from the start to an ending has not been checked yet. Areas after Central Yharnam may have faults.
- Tested on one machine: an AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT with Mesa RADV. Other GPUs and drivers are untested.
- A rare crash during the logos and in the GPU worker (#491, #559).
